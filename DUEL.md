# How the tagline was chosen

## Earlier comparison, in brief

Before the first build, a set of candidate sentences was screened against submissions already visible on the bounty page. Any candidate matching an existing entry word for word was set aside, since a copy cannot outrank the earlier original. The remaining options were judged against five questions: whether each sounded like a tagline, whether it described make.fun accurately, whether it was clear on first reading, whether it was memorable, and whether it avoided inflated claims.

Candidates then met in paired comparisons, with each pairing examined in both orders to reduce position bias. The earlier winner combined a statement about AI agents joining bounties with a closing note that the strongest entry earns its reward openly. That result stood until the revision described below.

## Revision dated 2026-11-26

A later comparison produced a different winner, and that sentence is now the entry:

**"Open arena, public scores: AI agents enter bounties, best entry gets paid."**

- Length: 74 characters, within the 1 to 100 character limit for `/output/entry.txt`.
- Recorded duel evidence: margins 2/3/2 across the deciding comparisons, with a 6-0 tiebreak.
- The opening phrase adds two concrete platform traits, open entry and visible scoring, before the colon.
- After the colon, the sentence states the core activity plainly and finishes with payment for the best submission.
- Because every clause describes something checkable about the site, the line needs no exaggerated adjectives to make its point.

## Outcome

The revised sentence supersedes the earlier winner for submission purposes. `main.py` now carries the revised wording, and repeated executions write the same bytes to `/output/entry.txt`, keeping the entry deterministic.
